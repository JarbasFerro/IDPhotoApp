# Calipic — Launch market prioritization

**Status:** Initial research recommendation  
**Reviewed:** 2026-09-17

## 1. Question

Which markets should most influence Calipic's launch brand, App Store presentation, document-profile priorities, localization, and early monetization testing?

The founder's preference is to go where the largest useful user base is rather than defaulting to Spain or Brazil.

---

## 2. Decision criteria

Because Calipic is iPhone-only and uses low-cost paid credits, population alone is not a useful ranking.

Market priority should combine:

1. **iOS reach** — proportion and absolute number of potential iPhone users.
2. **Mobile willingness to pay** — app/IAP spending is a proxy for credit-conversion potential.
3. **ID/passport-photo task volume** — frequency of relevant document applications.
4. **Language fit** — cost and quality of localization/support.
5. **Document-rule feasibility** — availability and clarity of authoritative photo specifications.
6. **Printing relevance** — whether physical ID photos remain part of common workflows.
7. **Competitive intensity** — strong demand can also mean expensive discovery.
8. **App Store search fit** — local terminology such as `passport photo`, `ID photo`, `foto carnet`, etc.

---

## 3. Why the United States is the leading launch candidate

### 3.1 Large iOS share

StatCounter reports iOS at approximately **60.7% of U.S. mobile OS usage in August 2026**.

Source: https://gs.statcounter.com/os-market-share/mobile/united-states-of-america

For an iPhone-only product this is unusually favorable: Calipic can address the majority mobile platform rather than a minority segment.

### 3.2 Largest mobile monetization market

Sensor Tower's 2026 State of Mobile research reports that the **United States remained the largest mobile market by revenue in 2025, with consumers spending nearly $60 billion**. Its 2025 report also identified the U.S. as the leading IAP market.

Sources:

- https://sensortower.com/blog/state-of-mobile-2026
- https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-state-of-mobile-consumers-usd150-billion-spent-on-mobile-highlights

This does not prove willingness to buy ID-photo credits specifically, but it is strong evidence that the U.S. is a favorable market for testing a low-cost in-app purchase model.

### 3.3 Very large passport-photo task volume

The U.S. Department of State reports:

- **27,348,416 U.S. passports issued in FY2025** (including passport cards);
- **23,308,976 passport applications received in FY2025**;
- **183,170,240 valid U.S. passports in circulation in FY2025**.

Source: https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/about-us/reports-and-statistics.html

Not every application maps to a Calipic opportunity and some users obtain photos from acceptance facilities or other services, but the absolute task volume is large enough to justify serious U.S.-first optimization.

### 3.4 Language fit

English is already a launch language, so U.S. prioritization adds no new core localization burden.

### 3.5 Brand fit

The approved positioning — simple, beautiful, low-cost, private, no unnecessary subscription — is compatible with a U.S. utility-app proposition.

### Initial conclusion

> **Use the United States as the primary launch-market design and monetization lens unless subsequent competitor/search-volume research reveals a material problem.**

This is stronger than the earlier “US maybe” hypothesis, but still not a commitment to launch only in the U.S.

## 4. English-language expansion tier

English localization can serve several additional high-value iPhone markets with little language overhead.

### Canada

StatCounter reports iOS at approximately **68.5% of Canadian mobile OS usage in August 2026**.

Source: https://gs.statcounter.com/os-market-share/mobile/canada

**Implication:** extremely attractive iPhone penetration and no additional core language.

### Australia

StatCounter reports iOS at approximately **65.2% of Australian mobile OS usage in August 2026**.

Source: https://gs.statcounter.com/os-market-share/mobile/australia

**Implication:** attractive iPhone reach and English fit, although total population is much smaller than the U.S.

### United Kingdom

StatCounter reports iOS at approximately **51.5% of UK mobile OS usage in August 2026**.

Source: https://gs.statcounter.com/os-market-share/mobile/united-kingdom

Sensor Tower identifies the UK among important Western European mobile-revenue markets.

**Implication:** likely a strong early expansion market, but local passport/photo workflow and competitive search landscape should be validated separately.

### Recommendation

**Tier 1 English:**

1. United States
2. Canada
3. United Kingdom / Australia — order to be determined from category-specific demand and competition

This tier can share much of the brand language but cannot share document rules blindly. Each country's profiles require independent authoritative sourcing and testing.

---

## 5. Spain

StatCounter reports iOS at approximately **31.4% of Spanish mobile OS usage in August 2026**.

Source: https://gs.statcounter.com/os-market-share/mobile/spain

Spain remains strategically useful because:

- Spanish is a launch language;
- `foto carnet` / DNI / passport / residence-related use cases fit Calipic closely;
- physical printing remains directly relevant to the product proposition;
- product/rule testing can be performed locally;
- the existing project already has Spain-specific photo research.

However, the lower iOS share means Spain should not define the global brand or automatically become the primary commercial market.

### Recommendation

Treat Spain as an **important launch/validation market**, but optimize the global acquisition proposition around larger iPhone-first markets.

---

## 6. Brazil

StatCounter reports iOS at approximately **24.5% of Brazilian mobile OS usage in August 2026**.

Source: https://gs.statcounter.com/os-market-share/mobile/brazil

Brazil has major absolute population scale and Portuguese (Brazil) is an approved launch language. The Polícia Federal also reports substantial passport-service activity/revenue, confirming meaningful ongoing demand for passport issuance.

Source: https://www.gov.br/pf/pt-br/acesso-a-informacao/receitas-e-despesas/demonstrativo-emissao-de-passaportes

However:

- iOS represents a minority of Brazilian mobile usage;
- very low credit prices may be needed for local purchasing-power fit;
- Apple price tiers, tax, and localized willingness-to-pay should be tested rather than copied from U.S./EU pricing.

### Recommendation

Brazil is valuable for **reach and localization completeness**, but is unlikely to be the first market used to optimize paid conversion for an iOS-only product.

---

## 7. Current market sequence hypothesis

### Market Lens A — Primary commercial/design target

**United States**

Use for:

- first App Store messaging tests;
- first pricing/credit hypotheses;
- primary English screenshots;
- icon/category-recognition testing;
- first serious ASO/competitor benchmark;
- priority official document profiles.

### Market Lens B — High-iOS English expansion

**Canada, United Kingdom, Australia**

Use for:

- early profile expansion;
- English ASO variants;
- cross-market icon/message validation.

### Market Lens C — Launch-language strategic markets

**Spain and Brazil**

Use for:

- Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese localization quality;
- culturally different brand/color validation;
- pricing localization;
- photo/printing workflow validation.

### Later research

Potentially important markets such as Germany, France, Italy, Japan, Mexico, and others should be ranked once additional language expansion and profile-production capacity are defined.

---

## 8. Why this matters to the brand system

Market priority changes more than translation.

It affects:

- whether `ID photo` or `passport photo` leads acquisition copy;
- whether physical print is shown prominently in the first App Store screenshots;
- examples and document names;
- pricing display;
- color/cultural testing;
- photography casting;
- icon interpretation;
- onboarding defaults;
- support content;
- App Store keywords and subtitle.

The **core Calipic identity should remain international**, but launch assets can be market-specific.

---

## 9. Evidence limitations

The current ranking uses strong macro signals but is not yet category-specific market sizing.

Before final launch order, add:

- App Store competitor ranking by country;
- keyword/search-volume data for `passport photo`, `ID photo`, `visa photo`, `foto carnet`, `foto pasaporte`, `foto 3x4`, etc.;
- competitor pricing/paywall/ad-model comparison;
- review mining for user complaints and unmet needs;
- authoritative document/photo requirements and frequency by market;
- estimated addressable iPhone population;
- StoreKit price elasticity tests after beta availability.

Do not pretend macro mobile revenue is equivalent to Calipic demand.

---

## 10. Current decision

> **Design Calipic as a global product, but use the United States as the primary commercial launch lens; validate the same brand in Canada/UK/Australia, and use Spain/Brazil as first-class Spanish/Portuguese launch and cultural-validation markets.**

This recommendation should be revisited after category-specific App Store/ASO research.
