# Calipic — Founder discovery answers

**Date:** 2026-09-17  
**Status:** Source input for Brand Foundations and Brand Decisions Log.

This document preserves the founder's first structured brand-discovery answers so later decisions remain traceable.

## Answers

1. **What should Calipic ultimately be known for?**  
   A + E — easiest ID-photo app + best way to create/print ID photos at home.

2. **Desired feeling after finishing a photo:**  
   Satisfaction.

3. **How central is printing?**  
   A — fundamental; Calipic is partly about turning photos into real prints.

4. **Primary launch audience influence:**  
   E + D — people avoiding expensive photo shops + parents.

5. **Reasons to choose Calipic over a free competitor:**  
   It is simpler; easier/more intuitive; beautiful.

6. **Admired brands:**  
   Apple; Teenage Engineering.

7. **Admired app-interface direction:**  
   Use known iOS best practices rather than imitating a specific third-party app.

8. **Five required traits:**  
   Elegant; friendly; photographic; practical; sophisticated.

9. **Five anti-traits:**  
   Complicated; AI-branded; generic; childish; cheap.

10. **Premium character:**  
    B — quietly premium through quality and restraint.

11. **Personality scales (1 = first pole, 5 = second):**

    - Warm → Clinical: 3
    - Friendly → Expert: 2
    - Soft → Precise: 3
    - Playful → Serious: 4
    - Expressive → Restrained: 4
    - Consumer → Professional: 1
    - Familiar → Distinctive: 2
    - Approachable → Premium: 3

12. **Color preference:**  
    No predetermined color. Study the effect of each color on human perception before selecting one.

13. **Light vs Dark:**  
    Equally native to both.

14. **Visual personality outside the portrait:**  
    A — extremely minimal.

15. **Visual motifs:**  
    Registration marks and calipers are not desirable. Crop marks, calibration marks, framing, photo-paper ratios, ruler/measurement cues, and optical geometry are promising.

16. **Logo architecture:**  
    C — powerful standalone symbol with wordmark secondary.

17. **App-icon goal:**  
    A — instantly communicates “ID photo.”

18. **Promising icon concept:**  
    Face silhouette + crop corners.

19. **Meaning of `Cali-`:**  
    Calibration/caliper/measurement connection is useful conceptually.

20. **Voice:**  
    B — calm and concise.

21. **Visible “we” personality:**  
    Yes.

22. **Preferred camera guidance:**  
    “Move a little farther away.”

23. **Humor:**  
    Keep official-photo workflow straight.

24. **AI/computer-vision messaging:**  
    Technology should disappear behind the result.

25. **Privacy/on-device processing:**  
    A — one of the main brand promises.

26. **Marketing strength:**  
    Prefer stronger language such as “Passport photos made right,” subject to accurate claims.

27. **Monetization:**  
    First result from the tutorial is free; user also receives an initial free credit; after that, inexpensive credits are purchased.

28. **“No ads” as explicit promise:**  
    Open to it; evaluate whether it could have negative impact.

29. **Priority markets:**  
    Go where most useful users are located; United States is an initial hypothesis to research.

30. **Launch languages:**  
    English; Spanish; Portuguese (Brazil).

31. **Consumer-facing naming:**  
    `Calipic — ID Photos`.

32. **Feature naming:**  
    Descriptive, not branded.

33. **What is definitely not Calipic:**  
    Something colorful, crowded, confusing, or uninteresting.

34. **Conflict hierarchy:**  
    1. Native iOS familiarity  
    2. Beauty  
    3. Accessibility
