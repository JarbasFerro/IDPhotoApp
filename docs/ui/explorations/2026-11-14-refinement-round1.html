Refinement, round 1

Why the app does not feel designed yet, drawn as two ways to fix it. Both keep every feature and the teal, the mat, and the paper. Both apply the same discipline: one alignment (left), three text sizes instead of seven, one primary button plus plain text for the rest, no small grey footnotes, one kind of status mark, and the photo as the largest thing on screen.

A

Tidy

Today's screens, cleaned up. Same structure; the rules above are applied to each screen.

  • White cards become plain rows on the background.
  • The mat stays a rounded panel, now also on Home.
  • Verdict and unlock say one thing in one line.

For: least work, least risk. Against: still five different page layouts; it will look cleaner, not distinctive.

B

One table, one tray · recommended

Every screen of the journey has the same two parts: the mat fills the top edge to edge, with the photo or the page lying on it, large. Everything you read or tap is in one white tray below.

  • Your sheet already works this way and is the screen that feels most finished.
  • Home, Photo Check, Print, Digital Photo and Finished all take the same shape, so it reads as one app and one journey.
  • No cards inside cards: the tray is the only container.

For: a recognisable Calipic layout; the photo gets 50 % of the screen. Against: more work (each journey screen is rebuilt); long content scrolls inside the tray.
