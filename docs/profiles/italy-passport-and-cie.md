# Profile research — Italy passport and electronic ID card (CIE)

**Status:** Researched; search-based official evidence accepted (ADR-051).
**Date:** 2026-11-11
**Profiles:** `it.passport` and `it.cie`, both print. Rules are shared (ICAO), so this is one rule set and two profiles.

| Rule | Value | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Size | **35 mm wide × 45 mm high** (CIE: "minimum 45 mm" high) | [CIE photo acquisition](https://www.cartaidentita.interno.gov.it/en/request-cie/photo-acquisition-process/) |
| Face | 70–80 % of the photo height | [Consolato Rosario](https://consrosario.esteri.it/it/servizi-consolari-e-visti/servizi-per-il-cittadino-italiano/passaporti-e-carte-didentita/documentazione-necessaria-per-il-rilascio-del-passaporto/qualita-delle-fotografie/) |
| Head | 32–35 mm, chin to top of head (one consulate) | [Consolato Toronto](https://constoronto.esteri.it/en/servizi-consolari-e-visti/servizi-per-il-cittadino-italiano/carte-didentita/) |
| Eyes | **23–31 mm from the bottom edge** | CIE portal |
| Background | Uniform. CIE: preferably light blue, beige, light brown, light grey, or white. Passport (ICAO guidance): white with even light | CIE portal; [Polizia di Stato](https://www.poliziadistato.it/articolo/il-rilascio) |
| Recency, colour, expression | Within 6 months; colour; neutral expression, mouth closed | CIE portal; Consolato Rosario |
| Head coverings | Only for religious reasons | Consolato Rosario |
| Paper | High-quality photo paper | [Consolato Houston, ICAO guidelines](https://conshouston.esteri.it/en/servizi-consolari-e-visti/servizi-per-il-cittadino-italiano/electronic-identity-card-cie/linee-guida-foto-icao/) |
| Home printing | Not stated | ? |
| Editing (reference) | CIE: the photo must not be cropped to exclude the background, straightened, or retouched | CIE portal. Calipic offers editing anyway (ADR-051) |

**Calipic values:**

- head target 32–35 mm;
- eye line 23–31 mm from the bottom;
- replacement colour white for the passport; white or light grey for the CIE.
