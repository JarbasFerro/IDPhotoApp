# Profile research — Spain TIE (foreigner identity card) and driving licence

**Status:** Researched; search-based official evidence accepted (ADR-051). Spain DNI is in [spain-dni](spain-dni.md).
**Date:** 2026-11-11
**Profiles:** `es.tie` and `es.driving-licence`, both print, **32 × 26 mm (height × width)**. This is the same physical format as the DNI.

| Rule | TIE | Driving licence (DGT) | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Size | "Tamaño carné", with the requirements of the DNI rules (32 × 26 mm) | **32 × 26 mm** | [Policía Nacional TIE](https://www.policia.es/miscelanea/extranjeros/ciudadanos_venezolanos.pdf), [Portal de Inmigración](https://extranjeros.inclusion.gob.es/es/InformacionInteres/InformacionProcedimientos/Ciudadanosnocomunitarios/hoja041/index.html), [DGT Sede](https://sede.dgt.gob.es/es/permisos-de-conducir/) |
| Background | White | Plain ("fondo liso"); older pages say light | as above |
| Pose | As DNI | Frontal, head uncovered, no dark glasses or anything that hinders identification | DGT |
| Religious veil | As DNI | Allowed if the face oval is fully visible from the hairline to the chin | DGT |
| Recency | Recent | "Original and current" | DGT |
| Re-submission | — | Since 6 Nov 2024, no new photo is needed when DGT already holds a valid one | DGT |
| Home printing | Not stated | Not stated | ? |

**Calipic:** reuse the DNI geometry from [spain-dni](spain-dni.md). The replacement colour is white for both. The driving licence's "fondo liso" also accepts white.
