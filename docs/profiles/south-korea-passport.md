# Profile research — South Korea passport

**Status:** Researched; search-based official evidence accepted (ADR-051). **Flag: official pages say self-taken digital photos are unsuitable**; see §2.
**Date:** 2026-11-11
**Profile:** `kr.passport`, print.

## 1. Rules

| Rule | Value | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Size | **3.5 × 4.5 cm** (width × height) | Ministry of Foreign Affairs posts: [Xi'an](https://www.mofa.go.kr/cn-xian-ko/brd/m_753/view.do?seq=1035220&page=1), [Toronto](https://www.mofa.go.kr/ca-toronto-ko/brd/m_5389/view.do?seq=1344544), [Lebanon](https://www.mofa.go.kr/lb-ko/brd/m_10801/view.do?seq=1346456) |
| Head | **3.2–3.6 cm**, crown to chin (most posts; some older posts give 3.2–3.5 or 2.5–3.5) | as above |
| Background | Uniform white, no border, no shadows or reflections | as above |
| Recency | Within 6 months of applying | as above |
| Clothing | No uniforms, military uniforms, or white clothing | as above |
| Editing (reference) | Photoshop-edited photos and photos with an erased background or a person composited onto white are not accepted | as above. Calipic offers editing anyway (ADR-051) |

## 2. Who may take the photo

Several posts state: **"instant photos or digital photos taken by individuals are unsuitable as passport photos"** (즉석사진 또는 개인이 촬영한 디지털 사진은 여권사진으로 부적합). See [Guatemala](https://overseas.mofa.go.kr/gt-ko/brd/m_5678/view.do?seq=961614), [Chile](https://overseas.mofa.go.kr/cl-ko/brd/m_6525/view.do?seq=1075253).

This is not an editing rule. It says a home photo does not qualify, as in Germany and France ([06 §2](../06-markets-and-documents.md)). **Founder decision needed:** keep this profile, or replace it with another Wave A document.
